# Awesome Morocco Public Procurement · Marchés publics au Maroc

A short, curated list of official sources and free guides for companies that bid on public tenders in Morocco.
Une liste courte de sources officielles et de guides gratuits pour les entreprises qui répondent aux marchés publics au Maroc.

> Maintained by the team behind [SplitGlass](https://splitglass.com) (a free directory of open Moroccan tenders). SplitGlass is one entry among official sources below; pull requests for other free, accurate resources are welcome.

## Official sources · Sources officielles

- [Portail des marchés publics](https://www.marchespublics.gov.ma) — the official portal: tender notices, consultation documents (DCE) and results.
- [Secrétariat général du gouvernement](https://www.sgg.gov.ma) — official legal texts and the Bulletin officiel.
- Decree no. 2-22-431 on public procurement — the text that governs thresholds, procedures and the reference-price rules. See the [guide to the official texts](https://splitglass.com/blog/textes-officiels-marches-publics-maroc) for where to read it.

## Find open tenders · Trouver les appels d'offres

- [Open public tenders in Morocco](https://splitglass.com/appels-offres-maroc) — notices from the official portal with subject, buyer, location, estimate, provisional guarantee and deadline, each linked to the official notice. Categories: works, supplies, services. Arabic version: [طلبات العروض العمومية](https://splitglass.com/ar/appels-offres-maroc).
- [Forward procurement programmes](https://splitglass.com/programmes-previsionnels-marches-publics-maroc) — the programmes published on the official portal, by buyer.

## Glossary · Glossaire

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| RC | Règlement de consultation — how the tender is run and judged |
| CPS | Cahier des prescriptions spéciales — clauses specific to the contract |
| CCAG | Cahier des clauses administratives générales — general administrative clauses |
| BPU | Bordereau des prix unitaires — unit price schedule |
| DQE | Détail quantitatif estimatif — quantities and estimate |
| BPDE | Bordereau des prix-détail estimatif — combined price and estimate model |
| DCE | Dossier de consultation des entreprises — the full tender file |
