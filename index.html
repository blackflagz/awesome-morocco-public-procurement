Awesome Morocco Public Procurement

Official sources and free guides for companies that bid on public tenders in Morocco. Sources officielles et guides gratuits pour répondre aux marchés publics au Maroc.

Maintained by the team behind SplitGlass, a free directory of open Moroccan tenders. It is one entry among the official sources below. Suggestions welcome on GitHub.

Official sources · Sources officielles

  • Portail des marchés publics: the official portal with tender notices, consultation documents (DCE) and results.
  • Secrétariat général du gouvernement: official legal texts and the Bulletin officiel.
  • Decree no. 2-22-431 on public procurement: see the guide to the official texts for where to read it.

Find open tenders · Trouver les appels d'offres

  • Open public tenders in Morocco: notices from the official portal with subject, buyer, location, estimate, provisional guarantee and deadline, each linked to the official notice. Arabic: طلبات العروض العمومية.
  • Forward procurement programmes, by buyer.

Guides · Guides pratiques

  • How to answer a tender: notice, consultation rules (RC), file contents and submission.
  • Using the official portal: finding a notice and its documents.
  • Understanding the CPS and the CCAG.
  • The BPU (unit price schedule) and DQE and BPU.
  • Reference price and abnormally low offers (articles 43 and 44, worked examples).
  • Guarantees: provisional, final and retention.
